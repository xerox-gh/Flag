Four downs. Ten yards. Advance 10 yards to reset your downs. Reach the end zone for 6 points plus an automatic extra point. Every possession starts with a play call.

WASD / arrows Move your player
Shift Sprint (uses stamina)
Space Snap / juke with the ball
1–5 Pass to a numbered receiver
Q Switch defender
E Tackle / handoff / call for pass / hold to block
Esc / P Pause

In Team Control, you take over the receiver after a catch. Choose a position to stay locked to that role. You can throw once per play, from behind the blue line. Yellow marks the first down. Call a punt to trade possession for field position.

In Player Career, choose QB, RB, WR, TE, offensive line or a defensive position. Choose a separate offense and defense position before the match; your career position is selected by default on its unit. AI controls your teammates. On defense, chase the ball carrier and press E within reach to tackle. Your teammates tackle automatically. CPU quarterbacks run, throw, and can be intercepted.

Phone / tablet: drag the movement pad; use the on-screen SNAP, JUKE, SPRINT, PULL and receiver buttons.

30 offensive and 30 defensive calls. Original arcade flag rules: 11v11 with pocket blocking, no kicking minigame, no fumbles, automatic extra points, a running play clock, and games may end tied. Training repeats offensive drives.
